About

I'm Rohit, a B.Tech CSE student building toward AI/ML engineer and SDE roles at product companies. I care more about shipping something that runs in production than about the cleverness of the architecture behind it.

MedSave — my flagship project — isn't a wrapper around an LLM API. The retrieval, the drug-matching logic for combination drugs and dosage variants, and the safety rules are engineering I did myself; the LLM is used only where ambiguity genuinely needs judgment.
